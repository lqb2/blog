back in september, i heard chani nicholas talk in her weekly astrology podcast about the fall equinox and the aries full moon. and now i can't remember if she said this thing i'm about to say or if it just clicked for me but i think i'm gonna assume she said it... either way, the thing was this:

the fall equinox and aries full moon always happen near each other. and in some ways, they are very contradictory energies. equinoxes are about balance, the times of the year when the light and dark match. and as we cross them, depending on which one it is and which hemisphere you're in, the daylight starts to get shorter or longer. 

but full moons are about fullness, about something peaking. aries is the prime initiator of the zodiac. and, as an aries, i know that part of our gift is in starting shit. using our fire to make shit happen... and with a quickness! 

what really clicked for me this year is the tension between the fall equinox, the time of the year when darkness grows and winter creeps towards us, and the aries full moon, which wants to start shit... and right now. it totally maps onto how i experience that time of year. fall cozy time is beginning and i start to shift towards that slowdown energy... but it's also the back to school and post-summer work flurry (the "oh damn we only have 3 months left and we said we were gonna do all this stuff by the end of the year so that means we gotta move on our things now"). the vibes really feel like opposites to me and i have consistently felt the rub year after year. 

unfortunately, i don't think this insight is going to solve the tension for me (like some insights do). what i do think it offers me is more awareness about what's happening so i can be more thoughtful and less surprised about the dynamic when it's present. maybe that increased awareness will offer a little more ease. 

fingers crossed this checks out in 2027! if you're reading this now and wanna send me a ping about next year, plz do!
